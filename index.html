How to make a Peanut Butter and Jelly Sandwich

  1. Gather your ingredients:
    1. Peanut butter
    2. Jelly or jam
    3. Two slices of bread
  2. Spread the peanut butter on one slice.
  3. Spread the jelly on the other slice.
  4. Put the slices together and serve.
